# NailMuse UK blog research pack

Status: research complete; articles and production images not yet published  
Prepared for: `nailart.bookmylook.co`  
Audience: UK readers looking for practical nail inspiration  
Editorial font: Montserrat (site-wide)

## What this pack contains

- 30 non-overlapping blog briefs in [`blog-briefs.md`](./blog-briefs.md).
- One primary query, search promise, outline and UK editorial angle per page.
- Research sources for fact-checking and trend validation.
- A copyright-safe original hero-image brief and descriptive alt text for every page.
- A recommended internal-link route so the finished pages form useful topic clusters.

This is a research and planning pack, not scraped publisher copy. Source articles may be used to verify ideas and facts, but their prose and images must not be republished. Final articles should be original, attributed where appropriate, and reviewed before publication.

## Priority publishing order

1. Publish pages 1, 7, 12, 13 and 19 first. Together they cover the supplied high-opportunity terms: “nail art designs”, “beginners nail art designs”, “easy nail art designs for toenails”, “rainbow nail art designs” and short-nail intent.
2. Publish pages 2–6 on a rolling seasonal calendar, refreshing the visible “last reviewed” date only after a real editorial review.
3. Publish the colour and occasion clusters, then the technique and care pages.
4. Link every design page to at least one relevant beginner guide and one related colour, shape or occasion page.

## Image policy

- Do not download or republish editorial photos from Vogue, Glamour, Marie Claire, Who What Wear, Woman & Home or Scratch without a licence.
- Create original photography or original generated imagery from each brief. Do not imitate a named artist or reproduce a recognisable source composition.
- Obtain written permission and credit the nail technician when using a salon or creator submission.
- Store production files under `public/blog/<slug>/` as `hero.webp` plus optional step images.
- Hero target: 1600 × 1000 px WebP, under 250 KB where quality permits; card crop: 4:3; social crop: 1200 × 630 px.
- Alt text should describe the visible manicure and relevant context, not repeat a keyword mechanically.

## Shared SEO rules

- Use one primary keyword per page and natural grammatical variants in headings and body copy.
- Write in UK English: colour, favourite, personalised, organise.
- Add a concise answer paragraph directly below the H1.
- Give every gallery item a useful caption: colour, shape, finish, difficulty and who it suits.
- Do not claim a look is trending solely because it appears in this plan. Recheck at least two recent sources at drafting time.
- Medical or allergy claims must cite NHS or British Association of Dermatologists guidance and must not be presented as individual medical advice.
- Add `Article`, `BreadcrumbList` and, only when visibly answered, `FAQPage` structured data.
