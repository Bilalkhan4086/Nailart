# NailMuse SEO Content Guidelines

Use this checklist whenever writing or updating NailMuse content.

## Blog research source of truth

Before drafting or updating a blog article, consult [`../content/blog-research/blog-briefs.md`](../content/blog-research/blog-briefs.md). It maps 30 UK-focused pages to distinct primary queries, research sources, original-image briefs and internal links. Follow the copyright and production rules in [`../content/blog-research/README.md`](../content/blog-research/README.md).

## Core keyword priorities

1. **nail art designs** — primary site-wide topic.
2. **nail art design** — singular variant for specific ideas.
3. **new nail art designs** — grammatical target representing “nail art new design” and “new nail arts designs”.
4. **beginners’ nail art designs** — beginner and how-to content.
5. **rainbow nail art designs** — colour-led inspiration.
6. **easy nail art designs for toenails** — pedicure inspiration.
7. **newest nail art designs** — freshness and salon discovery intent.
8. **new designs of nail art** — supporting natural-language phrase.

The raw research also includes “nail art design new” and other reversed-word-order variants. Consider their intent, but do not force ungrammatical wording into visible copy.

## Page-level workflow

- Assign one primary keyword and one clear search intent.
- Put the primary term naturally in the SEO title, H1, opening paragraph and meta description.
- Add two to four closely related variants only where they improve the explanation.
- Include original value: difficulty, tools, suitable nail shapes, colour palette, wear advice, salon brief, source and update date.
- Link to the main gallery and at least two relevant trend pages.
- Use descriptive image alt text without repeating the same keyword on every image.
- Keep titles specific and unique; avoid competing pages for simple word-order variations.
- Write in British English for a UK audience.

## Quality gate

Before publishing, confirm that the page:

- answers the search query rather than merely repeating it;
- reads naturally aloud;
- contains no copied publisher prose;
- has a unique title, H1, description and canonical URL;
- includes accurate visible content for any structured data;
- avoids claims that a page is guaranteed to rank;
- has a current source date when it discusses trends.

The canonical marketing and keyword context lives in [`.agents/product-marketing.md`](../.agents/product-marketing.md).
